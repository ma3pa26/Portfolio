## Executive Overview: The Hybrid Edge

This repository consolidates two parallel tracks of technical and qualitative craft developed across three decades:

1. **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) AI Systems Evaluation:** Evaluating frontier foundation models for multi-turn instruction decay, cognitive drift, sycophancy, and runtime alignment. Grounded in 13+ years of clinical observation and qualitative error-interception.
2. **Editorial Systems & Industrial Prepress:** 25+ years managing high-density multi-script typography, complex baseline grids, Computer-to-Plate (CTP) imposition, and preflight quality assurance for major publishing houses.

The discipline required to catch a 0.5-point trapping error across a 400-page educational press run is the direct operational precursor to auditing frontier multimodal and conversational AI agents.
